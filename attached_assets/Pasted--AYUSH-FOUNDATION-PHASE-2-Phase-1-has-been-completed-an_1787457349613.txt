# 5. PRESCRIPTION DETECTION

The checkout system must automatically determine whether the cart contains prescription-required medicines.

Rules:

### If no prescription-required medicine exists:

Normal checkout should continue.

Do NOT force the customer to upload a prescription.

### If one or more prescription-required medicines exist:

The checkout must clearly tell the customer that a prescription is required.

The customer must be able to upload the prescription before proceeding.

Do not allow the customer to bypass this requirement.

The requirement must be determined from the authoritative product/catalogue data, not from a frontend checkbox.
