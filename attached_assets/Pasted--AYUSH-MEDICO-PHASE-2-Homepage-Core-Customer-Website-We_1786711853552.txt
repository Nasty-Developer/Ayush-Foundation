* The green treatment can include a soft green overlay, gradient, shapes, or accents rather than making the entire screen harshly green.

* Make the hero feel like the main visual identity of a real pharmacy brand.

### FLOATING WHATSAPP BUTTON

Add a persistent floating WhatsApp button for the customer website.

Requirements:

* Fixed floating position on the lower-right side of the viewport.
* Use the official WhatsApp-style icon.
* Green WhatsApp visual treatment.
* Clearly recognizable as WhatsApp.
* Opens WhatsApp to **+91 98332 73838**.
* Works on mobile and desktop.
* Has an accessible aria-label such as "Chat with Ayush Medico on WhatsApp".
* Does not cover important page controls or content.
* Add appropriate safe-area/bottom spacing on mobile.
* Use a subtle hover/focus animation, but do not make it distracting.

The floating button should remain available while scrolling through the customer-facing pages.

### PREMIUM VISUALS + PROMOTIONAL BANNERS

Make the website significantly more visual and polished.

Use high-quality, pharmacy/healthcare-relevant imagery throughout the homepage instead of making the entire website text-and-card heavy.

Use images for appropriate sections such as:

* Hero

* Home delivery
* Healthcare products

Images must look clean, professional, modern, and relevant to a real pharmacy website.

Do not use random unrelated stock images, low-quality images, watermarked images, or misleading medical imagery.

### HERO / PROMOTIONAL SLIDER

Add a premium responsive promotional banner carousel near the top of the homepage, visually similar to polished e-commerce promotional banners.

The carousel should support multiple slides, for example:

* Medicine availability / easy inquiry
* Home delivery
* New medicine arrivals
* Healthcare products
* Pharmacy services

Each slide should support:

* Large background or side image
* Short headline
* Supporting text
* One clear CTA
* Optional secondary CTA
* Slide indicators
* Previous/next controls
* Smooth, subtle transitions

Make the banners feel like intentional promotional campaigns rather than generic image sliders.

The carousel must be:

* Fully responsive
* Touch/swipe friendly on mobile
* Accessible
* Optimized so images do not cause layout shifts
* Visually strong without becoming cluttered

For this phase, promotional content may use local mock/static data. Structure the carousel so that later phases can make banners dynamic through Firebase/Admin management.

Do NOT create Firebase banner management in Phase 2.

Do NOT make unsupported claims such as fake discounts, fake offers, fake prices, fake ratings, or fake medical results.

Use generic promotional messaging unless a real offer has been provided.
